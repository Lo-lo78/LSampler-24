LSampler-24 TEST72 - Stretch grain smoothing

Base: TEST70 (PREVIEW ROOT). TEST71 time/pitch experiment is intentionally discarded.

Goal:
- Keep the existing parameters and ranges unchanged:
  * Stretch Amount
  * Stretch Frequency
- Improve the existing granular stretch sound instead of replacing it with a time-stretcher/pitch-shifter.

DSP change:
- The previous position remap hard-wrapped at every grain boundary, producing discontinuities/clicks/buzz as Stretch Amount increased.
- Consecutive grains now use a lightweight 50% overlap-add.
- The overlap is crossfaded with a smoothstep interpolation whose two weights sum to 1, avoiding abrupt grain edges and avoiding the correlated-signal gain bump of an equal-power crossfade.
- Stretch Frequency still defines grain size.
- Stretch Amount still defines the analysis spacing/stretch factor.
- LFO Sample Move and FM sample-position modulation are applied coherently to both overlapping read heads.
- Stretch Amount = 0 completely bypasses the new overlap path and preserves the existing fast/original path.

No CMake/build/local compilation was run.
